Good to know

Questions before you ride

Ask us anything
Rider checklist · Tap to pack 0 / 5
Do I need experience to drive an ATV?

Not at all. Every tour starts with a safety and driving briefing, and the guide sets the pace for the group.

What should I bring?

Closed-toe shoes, clothes that can get muddy, a change of clothes, sunscreen, insect repellent and your ID or passport.

Can kids join?

Yes — children can ride as passengers, and the Side by Side tour is ideal for families of up to 6. Message us to confirm ages.

What if it rains?

It's the rainforest — rain makes it more fun. If conditions become unsafe we'll reschedule with you.

Are prices per person?

ATV and jet ski tours are per person. The Side by Side tour is per vehicle (up to 6 riders). Taxes are not included.
